We develop statistical methods for tensor data such as brain images, and for clinical data recorded at irregular visits, such as electronic health records (EHR). The lab is led by [Dayu Sun](https://www.sundayu.me) in the [Department of Biostatistics and Health Data Science](https://medicine.iu.edu/biostatistics) at Indiana University, with an affiliation at the [Regenstrief Institute](https://www.regenstrief.org/).

## Research

- **Tensor and structured high-dimensional data.** Methods for tensors (multi-way arrays) and other high-dimensional structured data that keep the data's multidimensional structure instead of flattening it into a vector. Neuroimaging is one main source of such data.
- **Intermittently observed data.** Longitudinal and time-to-event methods for data seen only at irregular clinic visits. In EHR, a main source of such data, measurements and diagnoses are recorded only when patients visit. Covariates are therefore measured at irregular times, and outcomes are never observed exactly, as with interval-censored survival times and panel counts.

The methods problems come from applied collaborations in Alzheimer's disease, mental health, juvenile justice, criminology, critical care, and clinical trials.
